One sentence, at most 200 characters: an absurd clause hidden deep inside
a terms-of-service agreement. A deadpan comedy editor judges the clauses
two at a time — the line must sound like real legal boilerplate until the
absurdity hits, with a concrete absurd image and no explaining its own joke.

**The line** (118 characters):

> The user grants the Company an irrevocable licence to their shadow, including the right to sublet it on overcast days.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) prints the line and writes the
exact same bytes to `/output/entry.txt`, the path the kit's gate scorer
reads. `entry.txt` holds the line exactly as submitted.
